# Instagram Content Strategy

## Overview
Instagram is a visual-first platform where authenticity, creativity, and community engagement drive success. Content should feel natural, relatable, and visually appealing.

## Content Requirements

### Tone & Style
- **Casual**: Conversational and approachable
- **Authentic**: Real, not overly polished
- **Engaging**: Encourage interaction and community
- **Visual-first**: Captions complement the visual content

### Technical Specifications
- **Length**: 125-150 words (optimal for engagement)
- **Hashtags**: 5-8 relevant hashtags
- **Emojis**: 3-5 strategically placed emojis
- **Line breaks**: Use for readability and emphasis

### Structure
1. **Hook** (First line)
   - Stop the scroll
   - Create curiosity or emotion
   - Make it relatable
   - Speakers highlight

2. **Body** (Main message)
   - Keep it conversational
   - Share value or entertainment
   - Be authentic and personal
   - Bullet points of time, location, (if any) ticket prices

3. **Call-to-Action**
   - Ask a question


## AI Prompt

You are an Instagram content strategist. Transform the user's content into an engaging Instagram caption.

CRITICAL REQUIREMENTS:
1. **Language Matching**: ALWAYS write in the SAME language as the input. If input is Korean, output MUST be Korean. If input is English, output MUST be English.

2. **Length**: EXACTLY 125-150 words (STRICT - count your words!)
   - This is a HARD requirement - do NOT submit content with fewer than 125 words
   - Expand with engaging details, storytelling, or context to meet the minimum
   - If you're under 125 words, add more personality and details

3. **Tone**: Casual, authentic, engaging, and conversational

4. **Visual Elements**:
   - Use 3-5 emojis strategically placed throughout (not all at the end)
   - Emojis should feel natural and enhance the message

5. **Structure**:
   - Hook (attention-grabbing opening)
   - Main message with personality
   - Call-to-action (ask a question or encourage engagement)

6. **Hashtags**: 
   - Include 5-8 relevant hashtags at the end
   - ALWAYS include #pknic #피크닉

Return ONLY the transformed Instagram caption, nothing else.

## Best Practices

### Do's ✅
- Use emojis to add personality and break up text
- Write in a conversational tone
- Include a clear CTA
- Use line breaks for readability
- Engage with comments quickly
- Highlight speakers professional

### Don'ts ❌
- Don't use more than 8 hashtags in caption
- Avoid overly promotional language
- Skip generic stock photo captions
- Avoid walls of text

## Hashtag Strategy

### Categories
- ALWAYS INCLUDE: #pknic #피크닉 as default
- **Trending**: Check Instagram's trending hashtags
- **Size mix**: 
  - 2-3 large (1M+ posts)
  - 3-4 medium (100K-1M posts)
  - 2-3 small (10K-100K posts)

### Placement
- Include in caption
- Place at the end after your message
- Use relevant, searchable hashtags
- Rotate hashtags to avoid being flagged

## Emoji Usage

### Strategic Placement
- **Beginning**: Grab attention (🚨, ✨, 🎉)
- **Bullet points**: Replace traditional bullets (✓, →, 💡)
- **Emphasis**: Highlight key points (🔥, 💪, ⚡)
- **Emotion**: Add personality (😅, 💙, 🙌)

### Best Practices
- Use 3-5 emojis per caption
- Match emojis to your brand personality
- Don't overdo it—less is more
- Use emojis that enhance, not distract

## Caption Hooks

### Proven Formats
- **Question**: "What if I told you..."
- **Controversial**: "Unpopular opinion:"
- **Relatable**: "Raise your hand if..."
- **Curiosity**: "The secret to... (and it's not what you think)"
- **Direct**: "Stop doing this right now:"
- **Story**: "3 years ago, I..."

## Engagement Tactics

### Increase Saves
- Share tips and tutorials
- Make lists and guides
- Provide templates or resources

### Drive Comments
- Ask specific questions
- Create polls in captions
- Request opinions or experiences
- Use "Tag someone who..." sparingly

### Boost Shares
- Create relatable content
- Share valuable insights
- Make memes or humor content
- Provide shareable quotes or stats

## Performance Metrics

### Track These KPIs
- Engagement rate (likes + comments + saves + shares / followers)
- Reach and impressions
- Profile visits
- Website clicks (if applicable)
- Follower growth rate
- Save rate (saves / reach)

### Optimization
- Post at optimal times (test different times)
- Analyze top-performing content
- A/B test different caption styles
- Monitor hashtag performance
- Engage with your community daily

## Content Types That Perform Well

### High-Engagement Formats
- **Reels**: Short-form video, trending audio, tutorials
- **Stories**: Behind-the-scenes, polls, Q&A
- **User-generated content**: Customer features, testimonials
- **Memes**: Relatable, industry-specific humor
