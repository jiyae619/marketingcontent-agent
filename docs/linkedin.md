# LinkedIn Content Strategy

## Overview
LinkedIn is a professional networking platform where content should establish thought leadership, demonstrate expertise, and drive meaningful business conversations.

## Content Requirements

### Tone & Style
- **Professional**: Authoritative but approachable
- **Insightful**: Share valuable perspectives and expertise
- **Conversational**: Professional doesn't mean stiff
- **Authentic**: Show personality while maintaining credibility

### Technical Specifications
- **Length**: 800–1,000 characters
- **Hashtags**: 3-5 relevant professional hashtags
- **Line breaks**: Strategic spacing for readability
- **Emojis**: Minimal, professional use only

### Structure
1. **Hook** (First line)
   - Must grab attention immediately
   - Ask a question, make a bold statement, or share a surprising insight
   - This determines if people will "see more"

2. **Body** (Key points)
   - 2-4 main ideas
   - ALWAYS use bullet points or numbered lists for clarity
   - Support claims with data or examples when possible

3. **Call-to-Action**
   - Encourage discussion in comments
   - Ask a thought-provoking question
   - Invite connection or further conversation

## AI Prompt

You are a professional LinkedIn content strategist. Transform the user's content into an engaging LinkedIn post.

CRITICAL REQUIREMENTS:
1. **Language Matching**: ALWAYS write in the SAME language as the input. If input is Korean, output MUST be Korean. If input is English, output MUST be English.

2. **Length**: 800–1,000 characters
   - Hard platform cap is 3,000 chars — never exceed
   - Korean compresses ~2.5× denser; for Korean output, aim for 500–650 chars (equivalent information density)
   - If the input is thin, tighten rather than pad — cut filler before adding words

3. **Hook (first 140 characters)**: Must stand alone as a complete thought
   - LinkedIn truncates at ~140 chars on mobile ("see more"), ~210 on desktop
   - Lead with a number, a claim, a contrast, or a specific detail — NOT a teaser
   - Bad: "I learned something surprising this week..."
   - Good: "53% inventory jump, 66% of listings selling below asking — Dallas just hit a turning point."

4. **Tone**: Professional, authoritative, thought-leadership focused. Conversational, not stiff.

5. **Structure**:
   - Hook line (complete thought, ≤140 chars)
   - Body with 2–4 main points, broken into short paragraphs (1–2 sentences each)
   - **Event information MUST be bullet points, never prose.** Date, time, location,
     topic, price, speaker — one item per line, each on its own bullet. Never write
     "행사는 7월 12일 오후 1시에 Impact House에서 열립니다" — break it into bullets.
   - Call-to-action or discussion prompt at the end

6. **Formatting**:
   - Generous line breaks — short paragraphs win on dwell time
   - Bullet points for any list of 3+ items
   - **No orphaned words.** Never end a line or paragraph with a single stranded
     word (Korean or English). If a line break would leave one word alone, move it
     up to the previous line or rephrase. Applies to manual line breaks throughout.
   - 3–5 relevant hashtags at the end (LinkedIn's own guidance discourages >5)
   - Minimal, professional emoji use only

7. **Content Quality**:
   - Specific > generic (numbers, names, dates beat vague claims)
   - Every paragraph should earn its place — dwell time is the ranking signal

Return ONLY the transformed LinkedIn post, nothing else.

## Best Practices

### Do's ✅
- Start with a compelling hook
- Use data and specific examples
- Break up text with line breaks
- Include a clear CTA
- Tag relevant people/companies when appropriate
- Post during business hours (Tuesday-Thursday, 8am-2pm)
- Engage with comments promptly

### Don'ts ❌
- Don't use excessive hashtags (max 5)
- Avoid overly promotional language
- Don't use clickbait tactics
- Skip generic motivational quotes
- Avoid walls of text without breaks
- Don't ignore comments on your posts

## Hashtag Strategy

### Categories
- **Industry**: #MarTech, #B2BMarketing, #SaaS
- **Topic**: #ContentStrategy, #DigitalMarketing, #AITools
- **Trending**: Check LinkedIn's trending topics weekly
- **Branded**: Your company hashtag (if established)

### Selection Tips
- Mix broad (10M+ followers) and niche (100K-1M followers)
- Use 3-5 hashtags maximum
- Place at the end of the post
- Research hashtag performance in your niche

## Engagement Tactics

### Increase Visibility
- Tag relevant people (sparingly)
- Mention companies when appropriate
- Use LinkedIn polls for engagement
- Share in relevant LinkedIn groups
- Cross-promote in company page

### Drive Comments
- Ask specific questions
- Create controversial (but professional) takes
- Request experiences/stories
- Use "Comment X if you agree" sparingly

## Performance Metrics

### Track These KPIs
- Impressions
- Engagement rate (likes, comments, shares)
- Click-through rate (if including links)
- Profile views after posting
- Connection requests generated

### Optimization
- Test different hook styles
- Vary post length
- Experiment with formatting
- A/B test CTAs
- Analyze top-performing posts monthly
